# Initialisation dependence of trained EP networks

Generated by `scripts/init_dependence.jl`. 5 seeds, 4 XOR patterns, 50 random initial states per pattern; mean ± std over seeds.

`accuracy` is the fraction of initialisations giving the correct
answer, `consistency` the fraction agreeing with the most common
prediction for that input, `convergence` the fraction reaching
‖∇E‖ < 0.0001. `spread(all)` and `spread(modal)` are the
dispersion of the equilibria over all initialisations and over only
those that agree on the prediction (circular s.d. for phases, linear
for positions). `λ_min` is the smallest Hessian eigenvalue at the
attained equilibria: a local curvature check, not a uniqueness proof;
`n/a` means no relaxation met the residual threshold, so there was no
equilibrium at which to evaluate it.

`T` is the relaxation budget as a multiple of the published span
(40 for the Duffing networks, 20 for the XY network). The extended
row separates properties of the trained network from properties of
the integrator having run out of time.
